What we collect, and why

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game. We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, location, or microphone/camera access. There is no sign-up and no social login.

To provide leaderboards, save progress across sessions, and understand which parts of the game work well, the app uses PlayFab (a backend service operated by Microsoft) as a service provider acting on our behalf. Through it, the app processes:

  • A device identifier. On first launch the app creates an anonymous player record keyed to the device's identifier (SystemInfo.deviceUniqueIdentifier). This is used only to keep coins, unlocked items, level progress and high scores tied to that device. It is not linked to any real-world identity and is not used for advertising.
  • Gameplay and progress data. Scores, stars, level progress, coin balance and items owned — so the game can save and restore them and show leaderboards.
  • Basic gameplay analytics. Anonymous events such as "level completed" or "purchase started", used only to fix bugs and improve the game (internal operations). These events are not used to build advertising profiles.
  • Purchase records. If a parent completes an in-app purchase, the purchase is verified through Google Play Billing and PlayFab. Payment details (card, billing info) are handled entirely by Google Play — we never see or store them. We only record which item was unlocked.

Advertising — contextual only, never tracking

The game contains one optional ad: a "watch an ad to keep going" offer a player can choose after losing a run. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which appears on Google Play's Families self-certified ads SDK list. We configure AdMob for child-directed treatment with a G (general audiences) content rating before the ad system starts. That means:

  • Ads are contextual only — chosen by what the game is, never by tracking your child's behaviour or interests.
  • We do not collect the Android Advertising ID (the app removes that permission entirely), and no advertising profile of your child is built.
  • Watching an ad is always optional and clearly labelled; skipping it only means skipping the free revive.

We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not allow any data collected in the app to be used for behavioural advertising.

In-app purchases and the parental gate

The game has an optional shop (character skins, coin packs, content packs). Every real-money purchase is placed behind a parental gate — a simple question a young child cannot answer — before Google Play's own purchase flow opens. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing.

How the data is protected and shared

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We use PlayFab (Microsoft) as a data processor to store this information on our behalf; we do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own purposes. Aside from Google Play (payments and billing), PlayFab (backend), and Google AdMob (contextual ad serving, as described above), no other third parties receive data from the app.

Data retention and deletion

Because there is no account to sign into, data is tied to the anonymous device record. You can request that we delete the player record associated with a device at any time by emailing the contact address above; we will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data on the device.

For parents

This app is intended for young children and is designed to be safe: no chat, no data required to play, contextual-only ads (one optional spot, never behavioural), and a parental gate in front of every purchase. If you have questions or want us to delete your child's data, contact us at the email above. Parents have the right to review the personal information collected, to withdraw consent, and to request deletion.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above before the change ships.
