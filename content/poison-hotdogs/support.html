Quick answers

Common questions

My child lost their progress.

There is no username-and-password account. The game creates an anonymous player record for that installation. Clearing the app's data or moving to a new device can start a fresh local save. Email us with the device, player nickname (if one was chosen), and what disappeared so we can investigate.

A purchase did not arrive.

Send the Google Play order number from the purchase receipt and say which item is missing. We can troubleshoot the entitlement and point you to the correct Google Play refund route when needed. Never send card details.

How do I stop accidental purchases?

Every real-money purchase starts behind the game's grown-up gate before Google Play's purchase flow opens. For another layer, turn on purchase authentication in the Google Play Store app under Settings → Authentication.

Can my child play without watching ads?

Yes. The rewarded ad is an optional offer to keep going after a run. Skipping it skips the free revive; it does not block the game. Ads are configured as child-directed, general-audience and non-personalized.

How do I delete my child's data?

Open the Grown-Ups area in the game and choose Delete My Data. That asks the game service to remove the anonymous player record and then clears the local save. If the button reports an error, or you no longer have the app, email us. We may ask for the in-game player ID or nickname so we can find the correct record.

Read the full deletion policy.

What information can appear on the leaderboard?

A player can use a generated silly nickname or type an optional filtered display name. That name and the score may appear to other players. Please use a nickname, not a child's real name.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones and tablets through Google Play. The game plays in landscape. There is no iPhone or iPad version right now.
