When reporting a problem, the three things that help most are: the phone or tablet you are on, which game mode you were in, and roughly what happened right before it went wrong. A screenshot beats all three.

Common questions

My child lost their progress.

Progress is tied to the device rather than an account, because the game has no sign-up. Moving to a new phone or clearing the app's data will start a fresh save. Email us with the details and we will see what can be recovered.

A purchase did not arrive.

Send us the Google Play order number from your confirmation email. Refunds themselves go through Google Play — we cannot issue them directly — but we can unlock what you paid for and point you at the right screen.

How do I stop my child buying things?

Every real-money purchase already sits behind a parental gate, which is a question a young child cannot answer. For a harder lock, turn on purchase authentication in the Google Play Store app under Settings → Authentication.

Can I turn off the ads?

There is one optional ad: a "watch to keep going" offer after a run ends. Nobody has to watch it, and skipping it only means skipping the free revive. Ads are contextual, chosen by what the game is and never by tracking your child.

Delete my child's data.

Email us and say which device. We delete the player record within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on the device itself. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Is there a forum or community?

Not yet. A community space is planned for a later phase — see the forum page for where that stands. Email is the only channel today.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets, via Google Play. The game plays in landscape. There is no iOS version.
