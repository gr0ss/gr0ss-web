Don't eat the green ones!

Rexy the T-Rex is hungry for letters and numbers. The poison hotdogs have other plans. A silly catch-and-dodge learning game for kids about 6–8.

Pick a game

  • Rotten Rescue Baron Bratwurst gooped everything and took Rexy's buddies. Grab the clean condiments, skip the gooped ones, and beat the boss at the end of each of ten painted worlds to free a friend.
  • Snack Attack Endless hotdog chaos. No finish line. How long can you last?
  • ABC's Catch the alphabet from A all the way to Z.
  • 123's Count and catch, 1 to 99.
  • Practice Any game, no clock, no losing. Learn it first, then play for real.

Learning that hides in the fun

  • Letter recognition and alphabet order, A to Z
  • Counting and number recognition, 1 to 99
  • Gentle early levels that teach as you play
  • Later worlds that give a practised kid a real fight

Built for small hands

  • Big, friendly touch targets
  • Icons and audio lead the way — reading stays light
  • Silly, never scary
  • Stars, stickers, daily treats and dinosaur buddies to rescue and play as

For grown-ups

This game is made for young kids, which means the boring parts matter as much as the hotdogs.

No chat, no sign-up

There is nobody for your child to talk to and nothing to fill in. No name, no email, no location.

A gate on every purchase

The shop is optional, and every real-money purchase sits behind a question a small child cannot answer.

Contextual ads only

One optional "watch to keep going" spot. Ads are chosen by what the game is, never by tracking your child.

Where it came from

The name, the hotdogs and the whole premise came from the developer's nephew, four years old at the time. He described a game about a dinosaur and hotdogs you must absolutely not eat. We wrote it down and then we built it.

Poison Hotdogs is the first game from {{gr0ssTechLink}}, a one-person studio making small, silly, kid-safe games for Android.
