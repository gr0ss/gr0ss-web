Where it came from

A four-year-old had one wonderfully weird request.

When my nephew was four, he asked for a game called Stay Away From Poison Hotdogs!

That tiny joke grew into Rexy, ridiculous hotdogs, and entirely too much green slime.

It was made for him first. Now we hope it makes your family smile, too.
