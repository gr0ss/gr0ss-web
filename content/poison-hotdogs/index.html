Where it came from

When my nephew was four, he made one wonderfully weird request: a game called Stay Away From Poison Hotdogs!

That was the whole idea—just a name that made us laugh. Somehow, that little request grew into Rexy, ridiculous hotdogs, and entirely too much green slime.

It was made for him first. Now we hope it makes your family smile, too.
