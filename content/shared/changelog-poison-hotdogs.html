Version 0.9.0 Pre-launch

August 2026

We're getting ready for the Play Store! Here's what's new lately:

  • Daily Treat got tastier. Come back each day and play to grow your streak — and when you claim your prize, watch the coins pour right into your wallet. Every day has its own goal picture so you always know what to do.
  • The world map shows off your wins. Stones you've beaten now wear shiny medallions. And the very first time you visit, Rexy shows you exactly where to tap.
  • Survive levels feel like winning. The timer bar now fills up toward a star — and the star gets wiggly and excited when you're almost there!
  • Grabbing coins sparkles. A little spark pops right where you catch each coin.
  • A tidier main menu. Practice mode moved to the Pick a Game screen, so the menu is easier to read at a glance.
  • A fresh hello screen when the game starts, painted to match the rest of the game.
  • Settings got a makeover. Everything is sorted into friendly cards, with a Grown-Ups corner for parents.
  • Fairer dodging. Hotdogs only get you when they really touch you, and you can hear every bonk.

Thanks for playing — and remember: don't eat the green ones!
