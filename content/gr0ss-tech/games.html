Stay Away From Poison Hotdogs In launch prep

A catch-and-dodge learning game for kids around 6–8. Rexy the T-Rex and his buddies run through ten painted worlds grabbing whatever the round asks for — a letter, a number, a clean condiment — while slime-covered hotdogs try very hard to be eaten. They should not be eaten.

  • Rotten Rescue — rescue your buddies! Ten gooped worlds, a boss at the end of each
  • Snack Attack — survive the rush! No finish line, just a high score
  • ABC's — collect A - B - C, all the way to Z
  • 123's — count 1 - 2 - 3, up to 99
  • Practice — pick a game to learn, no clock, no fail

Big touch targets, icons and audio over reading, gentle early levels and a real challenge later on. No chat, no sign-up, parental gate on every purchase.
