Stay Away From Poison Hotdogs In launch prep

A catch-and-dodge learning game for kids around 6–8. Rexy the T-Rex runs through ten painted worlds collecting the letter or number the sign asks for, while slime-covered hotdogs try very hard to be eaten. They should not be eaten.

  • Story Mode — ten worlds, one goofy hotdog boss each
  • ABC's — catch the alphabet, A to Z
  • 123's — count and catch, 1 to 99
  • Freestyle — how long can you last?

Big touch targets, icons and audio over reading, gentle early levels and a real challenge later on. No chat, no sign-up, parental gate on every purchase.
