What to write about

  • Something is broken. Tell us the device, the game mode, and roughly what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
  • A purchase went wrong. Include the Google Play order number and we will sort it out.
  • Delete my child's data. Say which game and which device; see the privacy policy for what gets removed and how long it takes.
  • An idea. Kids come up with better ideas than adults do. This whole studio's first game is proof.
  • Press or partnership. Same address.

What we cannot do by email

We cannot issue refunds directly — Google Play handles payment, so refund requests go through the Play Store. We can help you find the right screen.
