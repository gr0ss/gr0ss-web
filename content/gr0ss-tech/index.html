Who we are

gr0ss tech is a one-person studio. Design, code, art direction and the parts nobody brags about — store paperwork, privacy policy, build pipeline — all run out of the same room.

The plan is not one hit app. It is a reusable game framework — economy, saves, progression, shop, parental gate, analytics — with a catalogue of games built on top of it. Ship game one, keep the engine, start game two faster.

  • Platform Android first, Google Play
  • Audience Kids roughly 6–8, and the grown-ups behind them
  • Team One founder, plus a lot of automation
  • Status Game #1 in launch prep

What we make

Games that teach sideways

Letters, numbers, patterns — taught by playing, not by quizzing. A kid should want another round, and the learning rides along.

Safe by construction

No chat, no sign-up, no personal data. Every real-money purchase sits behind a parental gate. Ads, where they exist, are contextual only — never based on a child's behaviour.

Painted, not placeholder

Every screen a player sees is hand-directed art in one visual language. If a button does not look like it belongs next to the rest, it does not ship.

Out first: Poison Hotdogs

Stay Away From Poison Hotdogs Coming soon

Rexy the T-Rex is hungry for letters and numbers. The poison hotdogs have other plans. Ten painted worlds, four ways to play, and one rule that matters: don't eat the green ones.

The name and the whole idea came from the developer's nephew, age four at the time. We built the game he described.
